Add to OBS: Sources → + → Browser Source → paste URL → set Width & Height to match your stream resolution → OK.
Pairings & Standings scroll automatically when content overflows. The two tickers are the same data as a one-line strip running across the frame: give that source the full width and a height of about 140 px, and place it along the bottom edge. Bracket & Hero Breakdown always fill the frame. Featured Match is a full-screen matchup — both hero cards and what is being played; add ?layout=banner for the slim lower third over a camera instead. Life Counter and Round Status have a transparent background so they can sit on top of your camera feed. All three update instantly — no 30 second wait.
The Broadcast frame is a full-screen frame around three camera windows — table in the middle, a player on each side — with names, heroes, life totals, round and format filled in from the featured match. Put it above your cameras; add &debug=1 once to see where each window sits and how big it is. &card=0 drops its card slot, and &cams=0 shows large hero artwork in place of the two player cameras.
The two Winner screens announce the left or the right player of the featured match: their camera shows through one half, their hero fills the other. Make a scene for each and cut to the one that applies; ?side=auto picks the player still standing once the other is at 0 life.
Round Status stays invisible until you confirm on the tablet that the featured match is over — then it fades in and shows how far the running round has got. Add ?always=1 to keep it up all the time.

The text your viewers read on the overlays. Switches live as well; ?lang=de on a single overlay URL overrides it for that source.
The look of the boards, the tickers and the broadcast frame. Switches live; ?theme=mpa, usurp, dark or light on a single overlay URL overrides it for that source. The Featured Match banner and the Life Counter badges float over the camera and stay dark either way.
Open card search
Search any card and put it on stream with one click. It shows in the card slot of the Broadcast frame and in the separate Card overlay.
